How to use this sheet

  1. Section 1 already holds the June 2026 chart (88 stops). Replace the sample student numbers with the real fee-paying students at each stop, and add free students, teachers and caretakers. Save as CSV from Excel and upload, or edit the text box.
  2. Enter the costs for these buses in sections 2 to 6, or choose "Enter the amount directly" in section 8.
  3. Set the revision rules in section 8: required profit, method (A is recommended), rounding and the largest increase allowed this year.
  4. Read section 14 (Checks). Fix every red item; recheck yellow items.
  5. Section 11 is the fee chart. "same" means the stop keeps its chart fee. Download Excel or PDF, or print, and get it signed.
